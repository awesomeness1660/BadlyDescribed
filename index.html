Badly Described

Movies and TV from six continents and a hundred years, described as unhelpfully as possible. Name the title.

Easy: plain plot, pick from 4 look-alike options, 5 hints, 100 pts.

Medium: vague plot, type it, 3 guesses, 3 hints, 200 pts.

Hard: deliberately useless plot, 2 guesses, 2 hints, 60 seconds, 350 pts.

Brutal: same useless plot, 1 guess, 1 hint, 30 seconds, 500 pts.

Each hint costs 20% of the base points. On timed levels, time left adds up to 50% bonus. Typos only count for long titles, and only barely.
